The journey

Five chapters so far.

  1. 012017 – 2021

    Learning to build

    NIT Kurukshetra · B.Tech, Computer Science

    Four years of computer science, and the moment I realised I loved the invisible parts of software: the systems underneath that just have to work.

  2. 022020 – 2022

    Data at enterprise scale

    SAP Ariba

    I started as an intern and stayed to build the ingestion layer for Prism, a data lake serving SAP Ariba’s business customers. It was my first real taste of large-scale systems.

    Lesson Reliable data pipelines are a product, not plumbing.

  3. 032022

    Getting packages to doorsteps

    Flipkart · Seller Fulfillment

    Everything that happens after you hit “Buy”: alerting the seller, printing the label, first-mile pickup, moving between warehouses and last-mile delivery. I helped orchestrate it, at India scale.

    Lesson Every service call is a real parcel and a real customer waiting.

  4. 042023 – 2024

    Keeping a billion-member platform safe

    LinkedIn · Trust

    I built the infrastructure that hosts LinkedIn’s content moderation models, and helped it grow from regex rules to machine learning to LLM-powered defences. In 2024, a year of elections around the world, I led my team’s civic integrity work across India, the US, the UK, Japan, Brazil and more.

    Lesson When the stakes are public, “mostly works” isn’t good enough.

  5. 052024 – now

    Making hiring smarter

    LinkedIn · Talent Solutions

    I first protected the job ecosystem from abusive listings and used LLMs to clean up job data. Today I build the integrations that connect LinkedIn to recruiting systems like Greenhouse, Lever and SmartRecruiters. That work powers LinkedIn’s Hiring Agent, so recruiters can do their whole job in one place.

    Now Making AI genuinely useful for the people who hire.
